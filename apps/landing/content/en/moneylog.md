# See where your money goes.

MoneyLog imports your monobank transactions, puts every purchase into a category and shows how your month compares with your budget.

**[Request an invite](mailto:oldfromkb@gmail.com?subject=MoneyLog%20invite)** **[How it works](#how)**

## What you get

- **Automatic import from monobank.** Connect a personal monobank token once. New transactions arrive through a webhook, and a daily check fills any gaps.
- **Categories without manual work.** Each purchase gets a category from its merchant code. Add your own rules for the merchants you see often.
- **Your own transfers are not spending.** Money that moves between your own cards is excluded from your totals.
- **Monthly budgets.** Set a limit for a category and see at a glance how much is left.
- **Cash and other cards too.** Add a transaction by hand in a few taps.
- **Your data is yours.** Export your transactions to CSV at any time.

## How it works {#how}

1. Get an invite and sign in with your Only Tools account.
2. Create a personal token at api.monobank.ua and paste it into MoneyLog.
3. MoneyLog imports your recent history and keeps it up to date.
4. Set budgets and see your month on one screen.

## Your token is protected

MoneyLog reads your statement only. A monobank personal token cannot make payments. We encrypt the token with AWS KMS before we store it, and you can disconnect it at any time.
