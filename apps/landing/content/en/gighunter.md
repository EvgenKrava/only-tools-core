## What you get

- **Stop refreshing the job board.** New jobs are checked on your schedule, from every 15 minutes to once a day.
- **AI scores every job.** Claude reads the description and compares it with your profile, so you see a score, not a flood of spam.
- **Alerts in your own Telegram bot.** Matches arrive where you already are.
- **A proposal in a minute.** In the per-job chat, AI helps you draft a proposal, an estimate and clarifying questions.
- **Templates you control.** Edit the prompts used for scoring and proposals.
- **Nothing kept for long.** Old jobs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

## How it works {#how}

1. Get an invite and sign in with your Only Tools account.
2. Describe your profile and skills.
3. Connect your own Telegram bot and your Claude API key.
4. Get scored jobs and reply first.
