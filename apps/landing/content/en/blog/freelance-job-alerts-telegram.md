# How to be first on freelance jobs

On freelance platforms a good job collects many proposals quickly. Those who reply in the first minutes have a better chance of being read. But nobody can refresh a page all day.

## Problem 1. You see jobs too late
Built-in platform alerts are either too rare or too noisy. The fix: check new jobs automatically on a schedule and deliver them where you look most often, such as Telegram.

## Problem 2. Too much irrelevant noise
Keyword filters let junk through and drop good jobs that use different words. It works better when the job description is read and compared with your profile, not just a list of words.

## Problem 3. Proposals take time
A template proposal is obvious. A good one addresses the client's task, gives a rough estimate and asks one or two clarifying questions. A draft tailored to the job speeds this up.
