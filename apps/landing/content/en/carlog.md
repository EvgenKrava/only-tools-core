## What you get

- **Your car's whole history in one timeline.** Date, mileage, cost, work done and parts. Search by any word.
- **Reminders by date or mileage.** Insurance, service, tyre change. The bell shows what is due soon or overdue.
- **Receipts that don't get lost.** Attach a photo or PDF to every entry.
- **Scan the invoice instead of typing.** Take a photo of the garage invoice and CarLog fills in the entry. You just check it.
- **Several cars, one garage.** Yours, your partner's, your parents'.
- **Works like an app.** Install it on your phone from the browser, no app store needed. Light and dark themes, English, Ukrainian and Russian.

## How it works {#how}

1. Sign in with your Only Tools account (email or Google).
2. Add your car to the garage.
3. Log services by hand, by scanning an invoice, or by importing old notes.
4. Get a reminder when the next service is coming up.

## Who it's for

Drivers who want to know what their car really costs, and anyone who will sell a car one day: a complete service history builds buyer trust.
