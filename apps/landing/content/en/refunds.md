# Refund policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026.

Only Tools apps are currently free. This policy applies to paid plans once they are introduced.

## Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel a subscription at any time in the app or by writing to {{email}}. Access stays until the end of the period you have paid for, and no further payments are taken.

## When you get your money back

- **Within 14 days of the first payment** for a plan, you can ask for a full refund for any reason.
- **If you were charged by mistake**, for example twice or after cancelling, we refund the extra payment in full.
- **If a paid feature did not work** and we could not fix it, we refund the unused part of the period.

After 14 days, payments for a period that has already started are not refunded, except in the cases above.

## How to ask for a refund

Write to {{email}} from the email of your Only Tools account. Include the date and amount of the payment and the reason. We reply within 3 working days.

## How the money comes back

Refunds go to the same bank card through monobank, in Ukrainian hryvnia. We start the refund within 3 working days of approving it; your bank may take up to 30 days to show it on your account.
