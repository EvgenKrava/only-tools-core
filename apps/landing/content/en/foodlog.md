# Count calories in three taps.

A simple food diary that finds the products on your shelf by their barcode.

**[Open FoodLog](https://foodlog.onlytools.click)** **[What it does](#how)**

## What you get

- **Scan the barcode instead of searching.** Point the camera at the pack, and FoodLog looks the product up in Open Food Facts, which includes many Ukrainian products.
- **Add a product once if it's missing.** Copy calories, protein, fat and carbs from the label, and it's in your catalog next time.
- **Your own dishes.** Build borscht or syrnyky from ingredients and FoodLog works out the values per 100 g.
- **Recent foods first.** Your usual breakfast goes in with three taps.
- **Targets that calculate themselves.** Daily calories via the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, or set them manually.
- **Stats and weight.** Weekly and monthly summaries plus a body-weight log to see progress.
- **Works like an app.** Install it on your phone from the browser. English, Ukrainian and Russian.

## How it works {#how}

1. Sign in with your Only Tools account (email or Google).
2. Set a goal, or let FoodLog calculate it from your data.
3. Add food by search, barcode or your own product.
4. See what's left for today and how your weight is moving.
